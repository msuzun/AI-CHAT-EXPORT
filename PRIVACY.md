# Chat Export for ChatGPT — Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026. Applies to version 1.3.1 and its local export features.

Chat Export for ChatGPT is an independent browser extension, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by OpenAI. ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of OpenAI. The service name identifies compatibility; this statement does not grant trademark permission.

## Information accessed and purpose

When you request an export, the extension reads conversation text, roles, titles, message identifiers, available timestamps, attachments and images on supported ChatGPT pages. It may scroll the page to load older messages. Selected/batch export may navigate the current ChatGPT tab to conversation links already available on that site.

The extension may request conversation information, your current session information and media from ChatGPT/OpenAI endpoints, using the session you already have. Authentication information is held in memory for the operation, not intentionally persisted by the extension. Images may also be fetched from their original source URLs. These network requests are needed to retrieve the content you chose to export; they are not analytics or advertising requests.

## Storage and sharing

- No conversation data is sent to servers operated by the extension developer. There is no analytics, advertising, telemetry or cloud-export integration.
- Export previews temporarily store conversation data in the extension's local browser storage. Data is cleared after export/cancellation, when the associated preview tab closes, or on the next browser startup. Preview data older than an hour is rejected and removed when accessed or during tab cleanup. If the browser crashes or the extension is disabled, cleanup may wait until it runs again.
- Downloaded files are saved to the location you choose. Clipboard export writes the requested text to the operating-system clipboard. Those copies remain under your control and are not deleted by uninstalling the extension.
- Settings such as theme, interface language and default export format use browser sync storage. Your browser provider may synchronize those preferences according to your browser account settings. Conversation content does not use sync storage.
- Installing the update removes old cloud connection credentials saved by earlier versions. Removing local credentials does not revoke any authorization previously granted on an external provider's website.

The extension does not sell or use personal information for advertising. OpenAI, image-hosting services, browser sync providers and the operating-system clipboard have their own policies and controls.

## Your choices

Exports run on your request. You can close the preview, remove downloaded files, clear your clipboard, change extension permissions or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes its browser-managed storage, subject to the browser's own data handling.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact/support link on the extension's store listing.
